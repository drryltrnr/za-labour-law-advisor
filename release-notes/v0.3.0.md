v0.3.0 — general worker-side skill, with reference files

For any South African employment dispute, worker-side. MEIBC/MIBFA are now sector modules, and the COIDA-specific exit rules apply only to COIDA claimants.

New, checked against the Acts and Gazette notices:
- Dismissal (LRA ss 185–195, 189/189A) and the 2025 Code of Good Practice: Dismissal (GN 3470, GG 53294)
- Unfair labour practices (s 186(2)) and the CCMA process: conciliation, enforcing, rescinding and reviewing awards (ss 135, 143–145)
- BCEA basics (hours, leave, pay, notice, severance, which forum hears a money claim) and the National Minimum Wage Act
- EEA: discrimination, equal pay, burden of proof, the 2022 harassment code
- Prescription Act: when a money claim prescribes and what interrupts it

Corrections:
- UIA ss 38–41 were repealed with effect from 19 January 2017 (Act 10 of 2016); UIA and UIC Act breaches are enforced through BCEA ss 64(1)(dA), 68(1), 69 and 73, as amended by Act 7 of 2018
- UIF benefit appeals go to the UIA s 37 appeals committees, not CCMA arbitration
- BCEA s 34(5) quoted in full; earnings-threshold exclusions corrected; an unlawful deduction is not a listed unfair labour practice
- TAA s 241: only a senior SARS official may complain to a tax practitioner's professional body

Structure: MEIBC/MIBFA/Pension Funds Act s 13A and SARS/PAYE/UIF detail moved to references/, loaded only when triggered (core SKILL.md about 16% lighter).

Install: za-labour-law-advisor.plugin in Claude Cowork or Claude Code, or za-labour-law-advisor.skill with Save skill. Both include the reference files.
