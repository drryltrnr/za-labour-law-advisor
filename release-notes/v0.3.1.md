v0.3.1 — corrections from a live affidavit audit

Each point was checked against the primary text before it went in.

COIDA:
- R19,451-type figures are "compensation payable", never a "floor"; the only floor in Item 1 is the gazetted minimum
- Item 1 part-month wording quoted from Notice 7349 of 2026 ("x number of days off/total days in month")
- s 63(1) quoted, with the four exclusions: overtime paid every month is regular, not intermittent; a determination made on R0.00 declared earnings is grounds to ask for it to be redone; plead a fully computed alternative where a component's inclusion is contestable
- s 32 says nothing about tax; the exemption is Income Tax Act s 10(1)(gB)(i)

UIF:
- UIC Act s 17 (offences) was repealed by TAA s 271 (Act 28 of 2011); UIA offences run through s 64(1)(c)/(2) and s 65
- UIA s 56(3) (as substituted by Act 10 of 2016) and UIC Act s 10(3): monthly declarations due before the 7th
- UIC Act "remuneration" definition set out; whether it excludes COIDA compensation is left as the worker's contention until the Fourth Schedule is checked

Pleading hygiene:
- Check the "as at" date of any statute copy: as-enacted copies of the UIA still print the repealed ss 38–41
- Don't call the user's own schedules "official", "certified" or "conclusive"
