- COIDA commencement: Proclamation Notice 306 of 2026, tranches on 23 January, 1 February and 1 April 2026; the new "earnings" definition (Amendment Act s 1(g)) held back
- COIDA Regulations of 6 March 2026: inspections, rehabilitation and return to work, dismissal reporting, record-keeping
- OHSA s 24 incident reporting noted as a separate duty beside COIDA s 39
- BCEA s 34A: labour inspectors enforce it for retirement funds since the January 2026 withdrawal of the variation notice — a third route beside PFA s 13A and LRA s 33A
- LRA s 198A: deemed employment and Assign Services v NUMSA [2018] ZACC 22 separated from joint and several liability under s 198(4)/(4A)
- Ubogu [2017] ZACC 45 added beside NWPL v NEHAWU [2023] ZALAC 12
- PAIA and POPIA s 23 requests added for getting the other side's records
